# New Landscape Signals — 1 November 2026

This note records newly connected signals from the expanded research pass. It does not convert announcements or statutory text into proof of commercial demand.

## 1. Government data governance and interoperability

**Observed:** Presidential Decree 25-320 of 30 December 2025 establishes a national data governance mechanism for public institutions and public-service entities. It defines data classification, source cataloguing and a national interoperability system. The decree establishes a secure interconnection network separate from the public internet. It states that public bodies must classify and catalogue their data to enter the interoperability system; users must obtain the source's agreement through the system for the requested purpose. Some duties take effect upon publication of the underlying classification and cataloguing references. [S23]

**Observed:** on 20 September 2026 the Council of Ministers ordered preparation of a further presidential decree for secure digital interconnection, sectoral bodies and inter-wilaya coverage. [S26]

**Not established:** whether all required technical references have since been officially published; whether the later decree was promulgated by 1 November; what the procurement and supplier model is; what role, if any, private technology vendors may play.

## 2. Personal-data accountability

**Observed:** Law 25-11 introduces:
- an activity register for the data controller;
- a corresponding register for the processor;
- an automated processing-operations log for controllers and processors;
- a data-protection-officer role;
- prior impact assessment for processing likely to create high risks, including use of new technologies;
- regional control/audit poles for the national data-protection authority.

The automated log includes lifecycle operations such as collection, storage, modification, extraction, consultation, use, transmission, matching/interconnection, encryption and destruction. Its implementation modalities are delegated to regulation. [S25]

**Not established:** the current implementation regulation, official DPO guidance, enforcement priorities, organizational readiness, compliance spend or demand for a software product.

## 3. Trust services and electronic identity

**Observed:** Law 26-02 establishes the legal framework for electronic trust services and electronic identification. Qualified signatures are assimilated to handwritten signatures; qualified seals and timestamps receive specific presumptions; qualified electronic registered delivery provides evidence of sending and receipt. Economic trust services require authorization, and Article 27 requires data collected by trust-service providers to be hosted in Algeria, with transfers subject to other applicable laws. [S24]

**Not established:** the current list of authorized providers, the operational status of the new national authority, service-specific specifications, pricing and actual adoption.

## 4. Sector deployment signals

- **Agriculture:** the National Agricultural Information System was launched on 29 June 2026 for updated agricultural data and decision support at central and local levels. [S31]
- **Health:** an APS report dated 11 April 2026 described a digital platform in Guelma for remote tracking of patient records, intended to improve information exchange between health structures. It does not establish nationwide coverage, system interoperability or production usage across the health system. [S32]
- **Public works:** new online services for contractor qualification/classification and approvals for private engineering offices were announced for the national digital-services portal in September 2026. [S33]
- **Manufacturing:** limited-sample research indicates Industry 5.0 adoption remains early and that cost, awareness, skills and technology/organizational readiness are barriers. [S34]

These signals establish activity. They do not establish vendor accessibility, technical maturity, uptime, user satisfaction or private willingness to pay.

## 5. Payment infrastructure

Bank of Algeria materials list the PSP authorization framework and a 2026 National Payments Committee regulation. The Bank's communiqué reports authorization for Djezzy Payment Services to constitute as a PSP, followed by a separate operating-approval step. [S35]

This may alter payment integration opportunities. It does not prove that the provider is operational or that APIs are accessible.

## 6. Language technology

NADI 2026's shared tasks cover five speech tasks and 11 countries; Arab Voices maps 31 datasets across 14 dialects. ELNER-DZ provides a public Algerian Arabic/Arabizi NER and entity-linking dataset, while further Algerian Darija entity-linking research appeared in 2026. [S27–S30]

This means the initial assertion that Algerian language resources are extremely scarce needs qualification: assets and benchmarks exist, but their representativeness, rights, quality and task coverage remain uneven. The opportunity may shift from raw data collection toward quality evaluation, coverage gaps, domain adaptation and production reliability.

## 7. Cloud, compute and local infrastructure

**Observed:** ARPCE publishes a general-authorization route and a directory of cloud hosting/storage operators. The directory displayed 11 March 2025 as its update date and marks four listed operators with a note that their regularization applications were under examination. It is a dated provider map, not a current licensing certification. [S37]

**Observed:** local market signals include AYRADE's 2026 public offering intended to finance data-center expansion; Icosnet's public-cloud and GPU-as-a-Service offer; AventureCloudz, a Djezzy/Algeria Venture/Taubyte developer-cloud initiative; and Djezzy's later partnership with Armonika on sovereign cloud and virtualization. [S38–S41, S45]

**Not established:** current GPU capacity and availability, production SLA, cloud authorization status for each service, pricing under real workloads, data location attestations, reliability, occupancy and paying-customer adoption.

## 8. Public-sector cybersecurity and procurement

**Observed:** Presidential Decree 26-07 requires public institutions, administrations and public bodies to create an independent security and data-protection structure, linked to the head of the institution. Its duties include risk management, audit, continuous monitoring, cyber incident reporting, training and coordination with procurement to include security and confidentiality clauses when outsourcing. [S36]

**Observed:** CERIST's 2026 consultations cover GPU compute, a SOC video wall, backup/restore, anti-DDoS and a 12-month managed cybersecurity service with audit, continuous monitoring and incident response. [S42–S44]

**Not established:** whether the cited contracts were awarded, which supplier won, actual spending, implementation success, renewal, or whether similar procurement is repeated across institutions.

## 7. New market hypotheses

1. Privacy operations and data accountability for organizations subject to Law 25-11.
2. Trust-service integration and evidence-preserving document workflows.
3. Public-sector data governance, cataloguing, quality and interoperability implementation.
4. Payment operations and merchant integration around an evolving PSP ecosystem.
5. Sector-specific digitization in agriculture, healthcare, public works and manufacturing.
6. Algerian language model evaluation and specialized NLP/ASR components.

None is promoted to a product decision until buyer, workflow, alternatives, access and economics are established.
